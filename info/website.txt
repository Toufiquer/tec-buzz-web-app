Build a premium, bilingual (Bangla-first with English support), mobile-first website for Bangladesh SMEs. Position TecBuzz as a Growth System, not only a website maker: Website + Lead Management + Tracking + WhatsApp + SEO + Automation.

2. DESIGN DIRECTION

- Use the supplied TecBuzz logo in header, footer, favicon and loading state.
- Visual language: white/ice-blue background, deep navy text, electric-blue/cyan gradients inspired by the logo, clean geometry and subtle pixel/connection motion.
- Premium but practical: generous whitespace, rounded cards, soft shadows, real dashboard/project visuals; no generic stock-laptop hero.
- Typography: highly readable Bangla and English; bold short headings and concise body text.
- Mobile first: a compact sticky/floating WhatsApp CTA; easy-to-scan pricing, forms and galleries.
- Motion: subtle reveal, card hover and light dashboard micro-motion only; keep performance fast.
- Trust: real portfolio, verified testimonials and accurate claims only. Never invent revenue or lead-growth results.
- Content mix: 70% education/problem/proof, 20% demos/case studies, 10% direct offer.

8. FAQ — UPDATED FROM info FOLDER

1. ২৪–৪৮ ঘণ্টার delivery কীভাবে কাজ করে?
Standard build starts only after advance payment, required assets and approved scope; custom/automation work gets a separate timeline.

2. Domain ও hosting কার ownership-এ থাকবে?
Ownership, annual renewal and managed-hosting responsibility will be explicit in the proposal and terms. Never use an undefined "lifetime hosting" promise.

3. কতটি revision আছে?
The package/proposal states included revision rounds. Revision improves approved work; a new feature/page type is a change request.

4. Content না থাকলে TecBuzz লিখে দেবে কি?
Content support can be separately scoped, while client business information and approvals remain necessary.

5. SEO কি ranking guarantee করে?
No. TecBuzz delivers agreed technical/on-page SEO work; ranking depends on competition, content, authority and search-engine factors.

6. Ads budget ও management fee কি আলাদা?
Yes. Advertising-platform spend is separate from TecBuzz management/service fees.

7. Website launch-এর পর support কীভাবে পাওয়া যাবে?
Support channel, maintenance scope, response expectations and recurring fees are defined in the package/proposal.

8. Custom feature চাইলে timeline/price কীভাবে নির্ধারণ হবে?
TecBuzz reviews the workflow, confirms written scope, then gives a custom quote and delivery plan.

9. Free Audit-এ কী দেখা হবে?
Speed, mobile UX, CTA, WhatsApp, forms, tracking/Pixel, SEO basics, trust signals and follow-up flow.

10. ২৪–৪৮ ঘণ্টার promise কি সব package-এর জন্য?
No. It applies to qualified standard builds. Lead systems may take longer; Automation/Enterprise work is scope-based.

11. Client asset delay হলে কী হবে?
The delivery clock pauses until the logo, copy, images, contact details and approvals are received.

12. Payment, cancellation ও refund rules কোথায় পাব?
Read Pricing, Terms & Conditions and Refund & Cancellation before confirming a project.
